Tangelo

Tangelo is a Shopify theme for drinks brands that sell by flavor. Every flavor gets its own color, and the store pours from one to the next: in the hero, in the cards and in the cart.

Version 1.0.0 by Aliasgar Sogiawala. This guide was generated from the theme files on 2026-11-03.

Install

  1. In your Shopify admin, open Online Store › Themes.
  2. Under Theme library, choose Add theme › Upload zip file and upload the theme zip file. Don't unzip it first. If you added the theme from the Shopify Theme Store, it is already in your theme library.
  3. Choose Customize on the new theme to start editing. Publish it when you're ready.

Tangelo is an Online Store 2.0 theme and works on every Shopify plan. Sections can be added to any page, and the theme supports app blocks, filters, selling plans, markets and the Section Rendering API.

Theme styles

The theme ships with one style, Tangelo. A style sets the color schemes, fonts and button shape in one go. Change any of them under Customize › Theme settings.

Color schemes do most of the work. Each flavor on the demo has its own scheme, and every section, slide, tile and quote card can pick one. Edit them under Theme settings › Colors.

Set up a store like the demo

  1. Products. Give each flavor a clear product image on a transparent background (a PNG cut-out). The hero, cards and pack builder are designed around cut-outs.
  2. Flavor colors. Add the custom.flavor_color metafield (below) to each product, and to each collection. Cards, the cart and the pack builder use the product color; the header dropdown and collection pages use the collection color.
  3. Collections. Create a collection of your flavors for the featured collection and the pack builder, and use the collection banner image for collection pages.
  4. Pages. Create an About page and a Contact page and assign the page.about and page.contact templates to them.
  5. Blog. Create a blog (the demo calls it Journal) for the blog posts section.
  6. Menus. Under Content › Menus, set up a main menu and a footer menu, then pick them in the header and footer sections. Give the main menu's top link (Shop on the demo) links to your collections: its dropdown shows each collection's first products. Links to pages or blogs show the header's Image for other links.
  7. Reviews. Install any review app that writes Shopify's standard rating metafields. Stars then appear on cards and product pages automatically.
  8. Customer accounts. The account icon in the header opens Shopify's account sheet, where customers sign in and find their orders without leaving the page. It shows when customer accounts are turned on under Settings › Customer accounts. Choose the links in the sheet under Header › Customer account › Account menu; Shopify creates a menu called Customer account main menu for it.
  9. Complementary products. The product page has a Pairs well with section for products that go with the one being viewed. Choose them in Shopify's Search & Discovery app under Product recommendations. The section stays hidden until a product has some.

Product metafields

Create these under Settings › Custom data › Products. All of them are optional; the theme falls back to sensible defaults.

MetafieldTypeUsed for
custom.flavor_colorColorThe flavor's color on product cards, in the cart, in search results and in the pack builder. In the header dropdown, the pour takes this color while a visitor points at the product.
custom.short_nameSingle line textThe big name printed behind the product image on cards and in the featured product section, for example Blood Orange when the product is called Blood Orange Prebiotic Soda. The product title is used when it's empty.
custom.subtitleSingle line textA one-line description under the product name on cards and the product page. Cards show the product type when it's empty.
custom.nutritionMulti-line textThe nutrition table on the product page. One fact per line, written as Name: Value. When it's empty, the block's own text is used.
reviews.rating, reviews.rating_countRating, integerStar ratings and review counts. Review apps fill these in for you.

Collection metafields

Create this under Settings › Custom data › Collections. It's optional too.

MetafieldTypeUsed for
custom.flavor_colorColorThe pour behind the collection's products in the header dropdown, the collection page banner and the collection's tile on the collections list. The dropdown uses its color scheme's accent when it's empty.

Languages

The storefront text is translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Hindi. To sell in more than one language, add the languages under Settings › Languages and publish them. Visitors then switch language and country from the globe in the header, or from the menu on phones. Both selectors can be turned off in the header section, and the footer can show its own. Text you type in the theme editor, such as headings and buttons, is translated under Settings › Languages › Localize or with a translation app.

Accessibility and motion

  • Everything works with a keyboard. The hero switcher moves with the arrow keys, and drawers trap and return focus.
  • Autoplaying slides pause on hover and focus, and have a pause button. Every animation is switched off for visitors who ask for reduced motion, and the Motion settings let you turn effects off store-wide.
  • Images take the alt text you set in Shopify. Decorative images, such as the floating fruit in the hero, are hidden from screen readers.
  • Color schemes are yours to choose, so check text contrast when you change them. Aim for 4.5:1 for body text.

FAQ

Why doesn't the free shipping bar show in the cart?
Check Theme settings › Cart › Free shipping threshold. The bar is hidden when it's empty, and for shoppers using a currency other than your store's, because the threshold is set in your store's currency. The bar only shows progress; set up the free shipping rate itself under Settings › Shipping and delivery.
How do I give each product its own color?
Add the custom.flavor_color metafield described above. Products without it use the card background of the section's color scheme.
How do I show different nutrition facts on each product?
Add the custom.nutrition metafield and fill it in on each product. The nutrition block on the product page shows it, and hides itself on products where it's empty. For the label facts section, connect each value to a metafield with the dynamic source button in the theme editor.
Where do subscriptions come from?
From a subscription app. Once an app adds selling plans to a product, the product page shows the purchase options. The Subscribe and save section only explains your offer.
Can I use my own product images in the hero?
Yes. Upload a cut-out under each slide's Product cut-out setting. The built-in can artwork is only used until you do.
Does the store work without JavaScript?
Yes. The menu opens, the product page shows a plain variant list, the cart page has an Update cart button, and the language and country selectors have an Update button.
How do I translate my own text?
The theme's built-in text is already translated. For your products, pages and section text, use Shopify's Translate & Adapt app.
How do I update the theme?
When a new version is out, Shopify shows it in your theme library. Updating adds a new copy of the theme with your settings and content carried over, so you can check it before you publish. Code you've edited yourself isn't carried over.
